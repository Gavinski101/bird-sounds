Privacy policy

Bird Sounds · last updated 12 November 2026

Bird Sounds is a free, non-commercial app for learning Southern African birds by their calls. It has no ads, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing.

Without an account

Your progress, recording ratings, flags and settings are stored only in your browser on your device. They never leave the device unless you copy the transfer code yourself.

With an account

If you sign in (with email and password, or with Google), the app stores:

  • your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google, to identify your account;
  • your learning progress, recording ratings, flags and app settings, so they stay the same on all your devices.

This is stored with Supabase, the service that runs the app's accounts and database. Each person can only read and change their own data. Passwords are handled by Supabase and are never visible to the app's owner. With Google sign-in, the app only receives your name and email address from Google, nothing else from your Google account.

Other services the app uses

  • Bird recordings play from xeno-canto.org and photos load from iNaturalist. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when they send a file.
  • The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Deleting your data

Without an account, clear the app's site data in your browser, or use Settings → Start again → Reset all progress. With an account, use Settings → Account → Delete my account: this permanently removes your account and the progress saved in it. If you can no longer sign in, open an issue at github.com/Gavinski101/bird-sounds/issues and it will be removed for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
