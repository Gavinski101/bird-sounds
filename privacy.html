Privacy policy

Bird Sounds · last updated 25 November 2026

Bird Sounds is a free, non-commercial app for learning Southern African birds by their calls. It has no ads, and nothing about you is sold or shared for marketing.

Without an account

Your progress, recording ratings, flags and settings are stored only in your browser on your device. They never leave the device unless you copy the transfer code yourself.

With an account

If you sign in (with email and password, or with Google), the app stores:

  • your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google, to identify your account;
  • your learning progress, recording ratings, flags and app settings, so they stay the same on all your devices.

This is stored with Supabase, the service that runs the app's accounts and database. Each person can only read and change their own data. The exception is shared totals, which never show who anyone is, and which only count people who chose to add their answers (the "shared totals" choice when you sign in, which you can change any time in Settings → Account; it is off until you turn it on): per recording (how many people flagged it, its average star rating, and how often people answered it right), which help choose recordings for everyone; and per bird (how many people find it hard, and which birds people mix up), which set how soon a bird comes up and which birds are offered as hints. The app's owner can also look at totals across the accounts that share (for example how many birds people master per day) to see how well the app teaches and to improve it. Passwords are handled by Supabase and are never visible to the app's owner. With Google sign-in, the app only receives your name and email address from Google, nothing else from your Google account.

Other services the app uses

  • Bird recordings play from xeno-canto.org and photos load from iNaturalist. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when they send a file.
  • "Near me" asks your browser for your location (the app and your browser both ask your permission first). It is rounded to about 10 km and sent to iNaturalist to find the birds recorded near you; the rounded location is then kept only on your device. Other regions never use your location.
  • The page font is loaded from Google Fonts, which can see your device's IP address.
  • The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Your rights

You can see your own data in the app at any time (Settings → Account, and the transfer code), change it, or delete it as described below. Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can also ask what personal information is held about you, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by opening an issue at github.com/Gavinski101/bird-sounds/issues. The app sets no advertising or tracking cookies; it only uses your browser's storage for your own progress and settings.

Deleting your data

Without an account, clear the app's site data in your browser, or use Settings → Start again → Reset all progress. With an account, use Settings → Account → Delete my account: this permanently removes your account and the progress saved in it. If you can no longer sign in, open an issue at github.com/Gavinski101/bird-sounds/issues and it will be removed for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. If a change affects what happens with your account's data, the app asks you to agree again the next time you open it while signed in; you can sign out instead and keep your progress on your device.
