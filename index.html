About

Sounds from xeno-canto.org, shared by their recordists under Creative Commons licences (credited on each bird). Photos from iNaturalist. Names from the eBird taxonomy, as used by Merlin. How common each bird is in the region you pick comes from the Southern African Bird Atlas Project 2 (SABAP2, Brooks & Ryan, FitzPatrick Institute of African Ornithology, CC BY 4.0, accessed via GBIF.org), whose lists include birds that are only heard, together with iNaturalist records. Habitats from AVONET (Tobias et al. 2022, CC BY 4.0), grouped and with a few habitats added.
